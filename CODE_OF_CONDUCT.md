# Code of Conduct

Maverick follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and respectful.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, behavior, and risk.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them.
- Keep security-sensitive discussions private until coordinated disclosure is complete.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing technical discussion.
- Public disclosure of unresolved vulnerabilities.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the maintainers through the same private channel used for project governance until a public contact is configured.
