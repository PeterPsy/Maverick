Maverick Browser

A tab for your agent

Start here. Your agent can choose profiles, posts, searches and videos across Instagram, X, Reddit, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube.

  1. Click Maverick Browser Companion in Chrome’s extension toolbar on this tab.
  2. Paste the connector address from Browser and choose Connect agent tab.
  3. In the Browser tab that opens or is reused, press Connect selected tab. Keep both this tab and the Browser bridge open.

The agent navigates this tab. The Browser bridge keeps its connection to Maverick alive. Companion reuses an open Browser tab in the same Chrome profile; a separate window is needed when Maverick is open in a desktop app or another browser.

Then ask Maverick what to research. The agent can navigate, read, scroll and analyze images, video and audio in this tab.

Use your usual Chrome profile and sign in to the platforms in Chrome. Posting, messaging and form entry are not available. Your other tabs are not shared.
