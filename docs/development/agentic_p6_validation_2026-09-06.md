   correctly rejects that inconsistent root/projection pair with

   reconciliation of the historical upgrade scenarios with the native
   connection contract. Removing the validator, weakening connection identity,
   or altering Codex's active certificates to make fixtures pass is not a fix.

   `test_builtin_effect_descriptors_match_core_audit_inventory` detects the
   App Store descriptor mismatch. The executable inventory additionally drifts

   P6 additionally closes the omitted display-schema integrity dependency;
   it intentionally does not regenerate trusted audit hashes. The exact
   closures and their read/mutation/leakage behavior need their own review and
   explicit audit revision before reauthorization and a full rerun.
